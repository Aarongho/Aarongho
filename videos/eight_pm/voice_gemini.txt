A scared 13-year-old boy telling a true horror story to his friends, first person, past tense.
Fast and breathless, never slow or theatrical. No long pauses between sentences.
Hushed and tense most of the time, sharp and punchy on the sound words (Click, Slam, Dong,

terrified whisper. Then the very last line snaps to a bright, high-energy, excited call to action.

[tense, urgent] At 8:01, my little sister's window was still open. [whispering] And something was climbing in.

[fast, storyteller] You see, in this town, at exactly 8 PM, every window and every door must be shut. [ominous] Or else. [fast] That's why school ends at 7. Pizza places stop delivering at 7:30. And last year, Mr. Barnes forgot to close his cat flap. In the morning, his house was empty. Even the cat was gone.

But last night, Mom and Dad were working late. My name is Eddie, I'm thirteen, and I was in charge of locking the house.

[panicked, fast] 7:58. I ran through the house. Front door. Click. Back door. Click. Kitchen window. Slam. 7:59. Bathroom window. Slam. 8:00. [slow, heavy] Dong. Dong. [hushed] The town bell. Outside, every streetlight turned red.

[whispering] Then I heard it. Knock. Knock. Knock. Something was walking down our street, knocking on every door. Knock. Knock. It stopped at our house. The doorknob turned. Locked. It moved to the window. Tap. Tap. Locked.

[hushed] Then, upstairs, I heard a creak. And I felt a cold breeze. [terrified] My little sister's window was wide open.

[panicked] I ran upstairs. Lily was asleep. [whispering] Ten long, pale fingers curled over the windowsill. Then a face rose into the window. Huge black eyes. A smile way too wide.

[realizing, faster] Then I noticed something. Its fingers stopped at the edge of the windowsill. It couldn't cross the window frame by itself. It had to be invited. And an open window is an invitation.

[determined, loud] So I grabbed the window and slammed it shut. Slam! [fast] It screamed and vanished into the dark.

[relieved, soft] I hugged Lily. We were safe. Then she woke up and whispered. [sleepy little girl, whispering] Eddie, I didn't open my window.

[narrator, scared whisper] Then who did?

[terrified whisper] Behind us, the closet door creaked open.

[suddenly upbeat, high energy, excited, fast] Like and subscribe for part 2!
